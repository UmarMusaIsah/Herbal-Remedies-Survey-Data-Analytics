# Herbal Remedies Survey — Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview

The Herbal Remedies Survey Project is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding the use, perceived effectiveness, recommendation behavior, and reported experiences associated with herbal remedies among surveyed respondents.

The project demonstrates a complete analytical workflow from digital data collection through data preparation, analysis, validation, visualization, and professional reporting.

---

## Project Highlights

- **60** respondents in the final analytical dataset.
- **95%** reported using herbal remedies.
- **92%** perceived herbal remedies as effective.
- **78%** indicated willingness to recommend herbal remedies.
- **37%** reported experiencing side effects.

> The reported effectiveness figure represents respondents' perceptions and should not be interpreted as clinical evidence of medical effectiveness.

---

## Project Objectives

The project was designed to:

- Identify herbal remedy usage patterns.
- Understand common reasons for herbal remedy use.
- Assess perceived effectiveness.
- Examine reported side effects.
- Assess recommendation behavior.
- Transform survey responses into meaningful analytical insights.

---

## Data Collection

Survey responses were collected using:

- KoboToolbox
- CommCare
- Google Forms

The responses were subsequently reviewed, prepared, and structured for analysis.

Interpretation

The findings indicate widespread use of herbal remedies among the surveyed respondents and generally positive perceptions of effectiveness.

However, the reported experience of side effects highlights the importance of safe-use awareness and informed decision-making.
