# Herbal Remedies Survey — Data Analytics Case Study

## 1. Background

Herbal remedies are commonly used as part of traditional and community-based approaches to managing health and wellbeing.

This project was developed to analyze survey data on herbal remedy use and transform the collected responses into structured, interpretable, and visual insights.

The project followed an end-to-end data analytics workflow covering digital data collection, data preparation, analysis, validation, visualization, and reporting.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

The project focused on the following objectives:

1. Identify the extent to which respondents use herbal remedies.
2. Identify common reasons for using herbal remedies.
3. Assess respondents' perceptions of the effectiveness of herbal remedies.
4. Examine reported experiences relating to herbal remedy use, including side effects.
5. Assess respondents' willingness to recommend herbal remedies.
6. Produce analytical outputs that communicate the survey findings clearly.

---

## 3. Data Collection Approach

Survey responses were collected using three digital data-collection platforms:

- KoboToolbox
- CommCare
- Google Forms

Using multiple collection platforms provided a structured basis for gathering and organizing survey responses.

The collected data was subsequently reviewed and prepared for consolidation and analysis.

---

## 4. Data Preparation

Before analysis, the collected survey data underwent a structured preparation process.

The preparation workflow included:

- Reviewing collected records.
- Checking data completeness.
- Identifying inconsistent entries.
- Standardizing variables and response formats.
- Removing unnecessary fields.
- Structuring the analytical dataset.
- Checking consistency across data sources.

The final analytical dataset contained:

**60 respondents**

## 10. Key Findings

The final analysis identified several important patterns in the survey responses.

The validated presentation findings include:

- **95%** of respondents reported using herbal remedies.
- **92%** perceived herbal remedies as effective.
- **78%** indicated willingness to recommend herbal remedies.
- **37%** reported experiencing side effects.

These findings were incorporated into the final analytical presentation.

---

## 11. Interpretation

The findings indicate that herbal remedies were widely used among the respondents and that a large proportion of respondents perceived them as effective.

At the same time, the reported experience of side effects highlights the importance of awareness regarding safe and appropriate use.

The results therefore provide useful descriptive insight into respondents' experiences and perceptions of herbal remedies.

---

## 12. Recommendations

Based on the findings, the project recommends:

- Promoting safe and informed use of herbal remedies.
- Strengthening public awareness of potential side effects and appropriate use.
- Encouraging consultation with qualified healthcare or traditional medicine practitioners.
- Improving documentation and monitoring of herbal remedy use.
- Using evidence from community surveys to support informed decision-making.
