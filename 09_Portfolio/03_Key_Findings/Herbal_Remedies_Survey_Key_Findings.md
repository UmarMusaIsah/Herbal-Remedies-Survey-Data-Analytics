# Herbal Remedies Survey — Key Findings

## Overview

The final analytical dataset contained 60 respondents.

The analysis identified several important patterns relating to herbal remedy use, perceived effectiveness, recommendation behavior, and reported side effects.

---

## 1. Herbal Remedy Usage

### Finding

**95% of respondents reported using herbal remedies.**

### Interpretation

This indicates a high level of herbal remedy use among the surveyed respondents.

The result demonstrates that herbal remedies were a common part of the experiences captured by the survey.

---

## 2. Perceived Effectiveness

### Finding

**92% of respondents perceived herbal remedies as effective.**

### Interpretation

The high perceived effectiveness rate suggests that most respondents had a positive perception of the effectiveness of the herbal remedies they used.

This finding reflects respondents' perceptions and should not be interpreted as clinical evidence of medical effectiveness.

---

## 3. Recommendation

### Finding

**78% of respondents indicated willingness to recommend herbal remedies.**

### Interpretation

The recommendation rate indicates a substantial level of confidence or positive experience among respondents.

This may also reflect the relationship between perceived effectiveness and respondents' willingness to recommend herbal remedies to others.

---

## 4. Reported Side Effects

### Finding

**37% of respondents reported experiencing side effects.**

### Interpretation

The reported side-effect rate is an important finding because it highlights the need for greater awareness of safe and appropriate herbal remedy use.

The result represents respondents' reported experiences and does not establish clinical causation.

---

## 5. Overall Finding

The survey findings indicate that herbal remedies were widely used among respondents and were generally perceived positively in terms of effectiveness and recommendation.

However, the reported experience of side effects highlights the importance of safe-use awareness, appropriate guidance, and continued documentation of herbal remedy use.

---

## 6. Key Analytical Indicators

| Indicator | Result |
|---|---:|
| Final analytical respondents | 60 |
| Herbal remedy usage | 95% |
| Perceived effectiveness | 92% |
| Recommendation rate | 78% |
| Reported side effects | 37% |

---

## 7. Key Takeaway

The central takeaway from the analysis is that herbal remedies were widely used and positively perceived by the surveyed respondents, while reported side effects demonstrate the importance of informed and responsible use.

---

## 8. Analytical Limitation

These findings describe the responses captured in this survey and should not be generalized to the wider population without additional representative research.

The effectiveness figures represent respondents' perceptions rather than clinical measurements.
