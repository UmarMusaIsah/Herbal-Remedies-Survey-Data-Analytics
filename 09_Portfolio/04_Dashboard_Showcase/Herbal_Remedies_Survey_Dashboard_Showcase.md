# Herbal Remedies Survey — Dashboard Showcase

## Dashboard Overview

The Herbal Remedies Survey Project includes dashboard outputs developed to transform the prepared survey data into clear, accessible, and decision-oriented visual information.

The dashboard development process focused on presenting key indicators, survey patterns, and analytical findings in a professional and easy-to-understand format.

---

## 1. Excel Dashboard

### Purpose

The Excel dashboard provides a structured analytical view of the survey dataset.

It was developed to support:

- Data exploration.
- Summary analysis.
- Key indicator review.
- Visual presentation of survey results.
- Initial analytical interpretation.
